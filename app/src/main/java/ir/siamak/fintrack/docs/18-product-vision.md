# Executive Summary

FinTrack is not intended to be just another expense tracking application.

Its long-term vision is to become the leading Personal Financial Management Platform for Persian-speaking users and eventually evolve into an AI-powered financial assistant.

The platform is designed with scalability, extensibility, and intelligent financial analysis as its core principles.

---

# Mission

Help individuals and families understand, organize, and improve their financial lives through technology.

---

# Vision

Become the most comprehensive Persian financial management platform with intelligent decision support powered by Artificial Intelligence.

- cannot control spending.

- have no budgeting habits.
- lack meaningful financial reports.

# Five-Year Vision

FinTrack aims to become the reference financial management platform for Persian-speaking users by combining modern software engineering with artificial intelligence.

The long-term objective is not only to record financial data, but also to transform that data into meaningful insights, recommendations, predictions, and automated financial decision support while maintaining user privacy and complete control over personal financial information.
