# Reports

---

# Overview

The reporting module is one of the core components of FinTrack.

Its primary purpose is to transform financial data into meaningful insights, helping users understand spending habits, income trends, savings, and overall financial health.

Reports are divided into Free and Premium categories.

---

# Objectives

- Financial Analysis
- Spending Analysis
- Income Analysis
- Trend Detection
- Budget Monitoring
- Decision Support
