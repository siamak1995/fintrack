# FinTrack Future Ideas

This document contains ideas, experiments, and long-term features planned for future versions of FinTrack.

These ideas are intentionally separated from the official roadmap. They may evolve, merge, or be discarded based on user feedback and business priorities.

---

# Financial Health Score

Factors

- Savings Rate
- Income Stability
- Expense Ratio
- Debt Ratio
- Budget Compliance
- Installment Status

# Long-Term Vision

The future of FinTrack is to evolve from a personal expense tracker into a comprehensive intelligent financial platform capable of understanding financial behavior, predicting future outcomes, and assisting users in making better financial decisions while preserving privacy and maintaining full offline capability whenever possible.
